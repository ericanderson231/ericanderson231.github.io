Patent

US 10,628,284 B2 · Issued April 21, 2020

System and method for bitstream decoding with compiler-generated syntax trees

A declarative language for describing serial protocols, compiled into syntax trees that decode bitstreams captured by an oscilloscope. It replaced hand-written decoders and cut the time to support a new protocol from 3 months to 2 weeks.
